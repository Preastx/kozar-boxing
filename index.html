About our club

Kozar Boxing Club is a team of specialised professionals with extensive experience in combat sports, and our priority is developing people through our passion. Here you'll find a range of sports and activities: Boxing, Kickboxing, Jiujitsu, Physical Conditioning and Yoga. Our goal is to promote a healthy lifestyle through combat sports. We also have a competition team for students who want to go a step further and reach a high level of performance. If you have a dream, at Kozar Boxing we get to work and put together the plans and strategies to help you achieve it.

Your training camp in Fuerteventura!
